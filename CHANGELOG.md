# Changelog

All notable changes to Vertex are documented here.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-29

- Initial commercial release.
- Astro 7 theme for consulting and advisory firms with home, services, service, industries, industry, work, case study, insights, type archive, insight, search, people, person, about, careers, contact, consultation, privacy, terms, RSS, robots, sitemap, and 404 routes.
- Services, industries, work, insights, and people collections that reference each other, validated at build time.
- Full-height, type-led homepage hero over the ribbed-glass background.
- Megamenus in one panel that slide between menus, with a photo preview for each service and industry.
- Case study slices under a rotating sector heading.
- Case study index filtered by industry and service.
- Insights for articles, reports, and events, with report downloads and event registration.
- Partner profiles with their engagements and writing.
- Four-step consultation booking flow with a day and time picker and a live summary.
- Announcement bar for the next upcoming event.
- Careers page with open roles filtered by team.
- Offices with local time on the contact page and in the footer.
- Header that is clear at the top of the page, light once scrolled, and hidden while scrolling down, and a single SVG logo file.
- Self-hosted Mona Sans, charcoal panels with a grainy ribbed-glass gradient, two-tone headlines, and a mint accent.
