Priya Raman is a Senior Partner and heads the Singapore office, which she opened in 2016. She advises technology and consumer companies on growth strategy, market entry and the use of AI in commercial functions. Much of her work involves helping European and American companies build businesses in Southeast Asia, and Asian companies expand into Europe.

Before Vertex, Priya spent six years in strategy and product roles at an enterprise software company in Singapore and Bangalore. She leads the firm's research on AI in operations and serves on Vertex's executive committee, where she is responsible for recruiting and the development of the firm's consultants.
