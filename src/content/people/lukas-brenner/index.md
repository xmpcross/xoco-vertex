Lukas Brenner is a Senior Partner in Zurich and leads the firm's Mergers & Acquisitions practice and its work in industrials. He has supported more than thirty transactions, from commercial due diligence through integration, mostly for chemicals, machinery and energy companies in German-speaking Europe. He led the integration of two specialty chemicals producers that delivered its savings target eight months early.

Lukas joined Vertex in 2012 after seven years in manufacturing, latterly as plant director for a components supplier in southern Germany. He trained as a process engineer and holds a doctorate in industrial engineering. He works in German, English and French.
