Amara Nwosu is a Partner in London. She specializes in merger integration, with a focus on leadership selection, organization design and retention through the first year after a deal. She has worked on integrations in chemicals, building materials and banking, and co-led the merger of two specialty chemicals producers across Germany and Switzerland.

Amara joined Vertex in 2017 from the corporate development team of a London-listed industrial group, where she managed integration for six acquisitions. Earlier she worked in transaction services at an accounting firm. She is a chartered accountant.
