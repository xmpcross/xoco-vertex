Thomas Reid is a Senior Advisor based in New York. He spent twenty-six years in finance roles, the last eleven as chief financial officer of two publicly listed companies, a specialty retailer and a mid-sized regional bank. In those roles he completed nine acquisitions and two divestitures and led three multi-year cost programs.

Thomas joined Vertex as a Senior Advisor in 2022. He works with chief financial officers and audit committees on capital allocation, cost programs and deal decisions, and acts as a sounding board for partners on how a finance function will read their recommendations. He chairs the audit committee of a nonprofit hospital system.
