Strategy work fails in one of two ways. Either it describes an ambition without saying what the company will stop doing to fund it, or it is so hedged that nobody can tell what changed. We aim for neither. A Vertex strategy names the markets you will compete in, the ones you will leave, and the few investments that matter most, with the economics shown on one page.

Our teams are small and senior. A partner leads every engagement and stays in the room for it. We work from primary evidence: customer interviews, win-loss data, competitor cost structures and the margin by product and channel that most companies have never assembled in one place. That evidence usually settles arguments that have run inside the leadership team for years.

We judge the work by what happens after the board meeting. Clients who ask us to stay help us turn the strategy into a funded portfolio of initiatives with owners, milestones and a monthly review, because a plan that is not on the calendar is not a plan.
