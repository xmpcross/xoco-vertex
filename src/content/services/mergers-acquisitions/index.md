Most acquisitions are justified on the savings and growth the combination will create, and many fail to deliver them. The reasons are rarely mysterious. Diligence accepted the seller's numbers. Savings were estimated top-down with no plan behind them. Integration started after closing, when the best people had already begun to leave.

Our M&A practice works across the deal so that the assumptions in the valuation are the same ones the integration team is held to. We test the target's market and plan with primary research, build cost and revenue estimates bottom-up, and start integration planning before signing.

After closing, we run the integration office alongside your team: day-one readiness, leadership appointments, the first hundred days and savings tracking until the savings are visible in the budget. On carve-outs, we design the standalone business and the separation so both sides keep operating without interruption.
