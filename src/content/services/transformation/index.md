Most transformations do not fail on strategy. They fail in the eighteen months after the announcement, when the initial energy fades, the business still has a quarter to deliver, and nobody can say with confidence whether the program is on track.

We design transformations to survive that period. Every program starts from a financial target and works back to the initiatives, owners and milestones that will deliver it. A small transformation office keeps value visible each week and escalates decisions while they are still cheap to make. Change management focuses on the roles where behavior must change, which is usually a few hundred people rather than the whole company.

We also tell leadership teams what to stop. A program with sixty initiatives usually has twelve that matter. Cutting the rest frees the attention and talent the important ones need.
