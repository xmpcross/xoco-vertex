Few companies lack AI ambition. Many lack AI results. The pattern we see most often is a long list of pilots, each promising on its own, none of them changing how the business runs. The models work; the work around them does not change.

Our Digital & AI practice starts with the decision a model is meant to improve: which asset to repair, how much stock to hold, which customer to call. We size the value of a better decision, check whether the data can support it, and build only where both add up. Then we change the workflow, the incentives and the systems so the model is used every day.

We apply the same discipline to technology spend in general. An outside review of architecture, contracts and the project portfolio routinely finds a quarter or more of run cost that can be removed, and that money is often what funds the next wave of AI work.
