Technology companies have moved from an era when growth was enough to one where investors want growth and margin together. That shift reaches into every part of the business: which markets to enter, how to price, how to organize sales and customer success, and how much to spend on research.

We work with enterprise software companies, technology services firms, telecommunications operators and media groups, many of them mid-sized and growing internationally. Our Singapore and London offices lead much of the market entry work, helping European and American software companies build businesses in Asia and Asian companies expand west.
