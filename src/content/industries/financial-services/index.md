Financial services is our largest sector by revenue, and the one where the pressure on cost is most constant. We work with regional and national banks, life and non-life insurers, and asset managers across North America, Europe and Asia, usually with the chief executive, the chief financial officer or the chief operating officer.

Our work tends to sit where cost, technology and customers meet: cost programs that do not damage service, branch and channel redesign, core system decisions, and the integrations that follow consolidation. We bring partners who have worked inside banks and insurers, and benchmarks drawn from more than seventy engagements in the sector since 2021.
