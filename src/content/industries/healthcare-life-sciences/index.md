Healthcare organizations face a problem that most industries do not: demand they cannot turn away, with labor and funding that do not grow to match. Our healthcare work focuses on making existing capacity go further, through patient flow, theater and bed management, rostering and the redesign of clinical pathways.

We work with public and private hospital systems in the United States, the United Kingdom and continental Europe, and with payers and life sciences companies on commercial strategy, launch planning and operating cost. Our teams include former hospital executives and clinicians, and every operational redesign is built with the clinical staff who will run it.
