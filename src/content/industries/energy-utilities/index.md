Energy companies are being asked to do something no previous generation of managers has done: roughly double their capital investment while keeping bills affordable and reliability high. That puts a premium on spending capital well and running existing assets for longer.

Our energy practice works with transmission and distribution operators, integrated utilities, water companies and energy producers in Europe, North America and Southeast Asia. We help them prioritize capital, move maintenance from fixed schedules to condition and risk, and build the data and AI tools that make that possible. We also advise boards on portfolio choices as the generation mix shifts.
