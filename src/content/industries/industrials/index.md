Industrial companies spent the last decade growing into steady demand. Many now face flat volumes, structurally higher energy costs in Europe and customers who want supply closer to home. The response usually involves hard choices about plants, portfolios and partners.

We work with chemicals producers, machinery and equipment makers, building materials companies and component suppliers. Much of our work in the sector involves mergers and carve-outs, where we cover the deal and the integration, along with footprint reviews, procurement and plant performance programs. Our Zurich office leads the practice, with teams in New York, London and Singapore.
