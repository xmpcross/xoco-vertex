Consumer businesses live on thin margins and fast feedback. A pricing mistake shows up in the weekly sales report; a poor store opening shows up for twenty years. Our consumer work is built on detailed, local data: store-level profit, basket composition, shopper switching and competitor prices.

We work with grocers, specialty and general merchandise retailers, and consumer goods manufacturers in North America, Europe and Asia. Typical questions include where to open, refit or close stores, how to price and promote without eroding margin, and how to bring down the cost of running stores and supply chains while protecting the experience customers pay for.
